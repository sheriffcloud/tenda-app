01 Trust & safety 5 QUESTIONS
Q.01 Where does my money actually sit while a job is in progress? −

In the escrow contract on Solana — not in a Tenda bank account, not in the worker's wallet. When you fund a gig, USDC moves directly from your wallet into a program-derived account that only the contract can release. Tenda has no admin key, no pause button, no sweep function.

Q.02 What stops a worker from taking the job and disappearing? −

Two things. First: nothing is paid until proof clears. If the worker walks away, the funds simply return to the poster after the auto-cancel timer (default 24h, configurable per gig).

Second: every worker has a public on-chain reputation — completed gigs, dispute rate, average response time. Workers with bad histories don't get hired. The market self-cleans.

02 Money & fees 4 QUESTIONS
Q.06 What does Tenda charge? −

1.5% protocol fee on settled gigs, paid by the poster. That's it. No platform fee on workers — they keep 100% of their listed payout. No subscription, no listing fee, no withdrawal fee.

Solana network fee per transaction: ~$0.0001. The escrow program adds nothing on top.

Q.07 Can I get paid in my local currency? −

Yes. Workers receive USDC by default, then swap to local fiat through any of the traders in the §7 corridors map. You'll see live rates inside the wallet — pick one, hit swap, money lands in your bank or mobile-money account. 31 currencies live

03 Disputes 3 QUESTIONS
Q.10 What happens if the poster and worker disagree? −

Either side can open a dispute before the auto-release timer hits. Funds stay locked in the contract. A small panel of randomly-selected veteran workers and posters reviews the evidence (chat log, proof submissions, gig spec) and votes. The contract releases based on the majority vote.

Tenda doesn't decide disputes. Tenda doesn't see your wallet. The dispute panel doesn't see your real identity — just the case file.

Dispute rate: 0.4% of settled gigs

04 Crypto & wallet 4 QUESTIONS
Q.13 Do I need to know anything about crypto to use Tenda? −

No. The wallet is created for you on signup, secured by a passkey on your device — same auth pattern as Apple Pay or Google Pay. You never see a seed phrase unless you ask. You never approve a transaction in MetaMask. The escrow happens behind the screen you'd use to "post a job" or "submit work."

If you do know crypto: yes, you can export your private key, connect Phantom, and interact with the contract directly. Both modes work. Self-custody by default

Q.17 My country isn't on the map. Can I still use Tenda? −

Yes — the contract doesn't care where you are. Anyone with a phone and an internet connection can post or accept a gig. Sticky rail = scannable

Five categories, never more. Active row gets accent-green left border + soft tint. Counts on the right ("5", "4", "3"…) signal scope at a glance. Bottom of rail: "can't find it?" docs CTA — keeps the rail useful even when you've already scrolled.

Default-open hierarchy

First Q in each category is open. Two more opened in CAT 01 (the most-asked: where's my money, and what stops a worker walking away). Everything else collapsed. This gives readers a content rhythm and a complete narrative without forcing them to click.

Receipts inside answers

Where a claim is on-chain, drop a .qa-receipt callout: program ID, verification status, links. Treat the answer like a short proof. The accent-green left bar matches the §3 escrow card vocabulary so the page reads as a single voice.

Voice

Direct, present-tense, one or two sentences before any list. "If we don't know, we say so." The questions themselves are written in the user's language ("What stops a worker from disappearing?") not marketing language ("How does Tenda ensure quality?"). This matches the §6 honesty pose.

Motion

Accordion: chevron rotates 0→180°, body height animates 0 → auto over 240ms with ease-out. Toggle pill swaps from + outline to filled green −. Sticky rail active state slides via ::before indicator to follow the visible category as you scroll (scroll-spy). Receipt callouts fade-up on first reveal.
