§ 06·WHY TENDA

Four reasons it works.
All of them measurable.

No mission statements. No vague pledges. Each pillar carries its own proof — a stat, a receipt, a comparison — that you can verify on-chain or against the public competition.

Pillar = proof, not poster

Each column leads with an artifact: a comparison bar (Speed), a real receipt (Proof), a fee chart (Cost), a dot-map (Borderless). The verb headline comes after the visual — viewer is convinced before they read.

Color = pillar, not category

Blue · green · orange · ink. Distinct from the §4 stage palette intentionally — these are arguments, not lifecycle states. Top hairline draws in on hover (0 → 100%) as a soft selection cue.

Honest comparisons

Cost pillar names actual competitors (Fiverr 20–25%, Western Union ~7%, bank wire $25+). Speed pillar names PayPal + bank wire. Risky in legal review, devastating in the deck — comparison is the whole job here.

Footnote receipts

Every pillar ends with a small mono-monospaced fact (1.7s · 30d rolling, OtterSec · Mar 2025, NGN · GHS · KES · …). Lets the bold claim cite itself without hero-stat repetition.

Motion

Pillars fade up 16px → 0, opacity 0 → 1, staggered 120ms. Top hairline scales 0 → 100% on hover (400ms, ease-out). Bars (P1) and fee fills (P3) animate width on first viewport-enter. Dot-map (P4) pulses one dot every 1.2s (rotating).

Spacing

Section 112 80 120. Header grid 1.2fr 1fr · gap 64. Pillar grid 4 cols · no gap (hairlines do the work). Pad 48 36 44. Vis cell height 140 for visual consistency across all four.
