SAME WALLET · SAME ESCROW · ONE APP

Identity by accent

Gigs = brand blue (#5E87E8), Exchange = accent orange (#F0A365). Top hairline + corner-glow + icon tint + headline accent + footer link all carry the color. No rainbows, no ambiguity.

Authentic mini-visuals

Gigs card embeds 3 real gig rows (cat chip + title + SOL + countdown — straight from home.html). Exchange card embeds the OfferSummaryCard scaled down (pill + payment-method chips + trade row + rate + meta). The viewer sees the actual product anatomy, not a metaphor.

Headline rhythm

Same shape on both: "Gigs that pay on proof." / "SOL ↔ local cash, without the middle." Accent-colored verb phrase at the end. Parallel cadence makes the cards read as siblings, not strangers.

Bridge caption

SAME WALLET · SAME ESCROW · ONE APP sits between cards on a hairline. Closes the section by collapsing the duality back into a unified product.

Motion

On viewport-enter: cards slide up 16px → 0 + opacity 0 → 1, staggered 120ms. Pulse ring on OPEN/LOCKED pills. Footer hairlines fade in last. No infinite motion — heavy products, calm motion.

Spacing

Section 96 80 112. Cards grid 1fr 1fr · gap 32. Card padding 36 · radius 28. Inner gig rows 14 16 · radius 14. OSC 22 · radius 18.
