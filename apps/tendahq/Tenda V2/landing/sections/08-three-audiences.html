Three sides.
One contract.

Native artifacts, not stock illos

Each column carries a piece of the real product UI: worker = locked-job card with payout + checkpoints; poster = my-gigs mini-dashboard with status pills; trader = orderbook strip on dark surface. The audience recognizes their own screen — that's the pitch.

Role color leads everywhere

Green / blue / orange repeat from §1 product spine through eyebrow, headline em, KPI value, and CTA arrow. Trader column intentionally inverts to dark — traders read tickers on dark surfaces, and the inversion creates rhythm in the strip.

Why three, not two

Most marketplaces hide their liquidity layer. Tenda's economic loop only works if traders are visible — workers cash out into local fiat through them. Surfacing traders here is positioning: this is a 3-sided marketplace, by design.

CTAs are role-specific

"Browse open jobs" / "Post your first gig" / "Open a trader account" — each routes to a different onboarding (worker browse, poster post, trader KYC). The §11 footer + §10 final CTA collect the indecisive into the single app download.

Motion

Tab strip pulses the active dot (2.4s breathe). Top accent rule draws on column hover (0.4s). Within artifacts: worker checkpoint bar fills 0 → 100% on enter; poster dashboard counts up KPI numbers; trader orderbook bid/ask rows shimmer left-to-right (1.8s loop) suggesting live data.
