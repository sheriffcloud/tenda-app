Composition

B-frame, A-content. Top ticker = live rates. Bottom ticker = recent community settlements. Stat row anchors trust facts mid-page. Right side = drifting 3D wall + one featured card forward.

Featured card placement

Card sits flat (not 3D-tilted) at left edge of wall, slight rotation only. Reads as "the front-of-store sample" while the 3D grid is "everything in stock." The blue glow border separates it from the wall behind.

Headline copy

The escrow does the trusting. Italic accent on "trusting." Carries B's confident-declarative voice. Ribbon copy in mono-13 for that trading-desk feel.

Motion · tickers

Top ticker scrolls right→left, 60s. Bottom ticker scrolls left→right, 75s. Pause on hover. Mask-fade at edges.

Motion · the wall

Each mini-card drifts ±7px / 7s staggered (delays -2.2s · -4.4s · -1.5s). LIVE pulse ring on eyebrow. Featured card glow throbs 4s. Reduced-motion → all static, drift removed, tickers static with edge fades only.
