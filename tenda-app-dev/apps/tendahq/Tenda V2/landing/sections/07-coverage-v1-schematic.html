Schematic ≠ careless

Continents are abstract rounded blobs, not a real Mercator outline. The map's job is to locate the dots, not to be a geography lesson. Real-map fidelity invites pedantic critique ("Madagascar is missing"); abstraction sidesteps it while keeping the spatial story.

Honest scope

Six live, four coming with quarters. No "globally available" weasel-claim. The "Need a market we don't list?" link in the sidebar is the single CTA — turns gaps into signal collection.

Corridors as proof of life

Dashed green arcs only between live markets. Pulls "we settle here" into "we settle between these." Top-corridor stat (NGN ↔ PHP) anchors the headline arc.

Sidebar = receipts

Every market gets a flag, currency code, city, and status pill — same anatomy as §5 ticker rows. Footer carries 22 corridors / top corridor / largest trade — three numbers that feel caught.

Motion

Continents fade in 0 → 1 over 400ms. Live dots scale-in with double-ring pulse (rings 0.30 → 0 alpha, 1.6s loop, staggered +200ms each). Corridor arcs draw stroke-dashoffset over 1.4s on viewport-enter. Coming dots fade in last (no pulse).
