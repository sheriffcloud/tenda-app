The closer

Last beat of the page. Oversized 96px headline, two action verbs in the hierarchy ("waiting" struck-thru by tone, "earning" in accent green). The third line ("Or hiring. Or trading.") opens the door for non-worker audiences without diluting the primary push.

Real Android frame, real screen

Pulled the android_frame.jsx starter, but bypassed its default app bar — rendered the actual Tenda home screen inside instead: balance card with green accent glow, 4 categories, "Funds locked" gig at the top of the feed, custom tab bar with center FAB. Three floating annotations point at the live UI to anchor the proof in product reality.

Conversion stack

Headline → sub → primary CTA (Play / App Store) → fallback (QR for desktop visitors) → trust receipts (settlement, fee, dispute rate). Five rungs from "I'm intrigued" to "I'm installing." QR card is hidden on mobile (you're already there).

Receipts return

The same three numbers from §6 + §3 reappear here as the final reassurance: 1.7s avg settle, 1.5% fee, 0.4% disputes. Every claim already made on the page lands once more before the user sees the download buttons. Repetition is conviction.

Motion

Eyebrow pulse loops 2.4s. Headline fades up with 50ms word stagger. Phone frame rises with 0.3s rotateY tilt-in. Annotations slide in from outside-in (200ms stagger) connecting to phone via animated dashed line. Within app: balance number counts up from 0; "Funds locked" pill pulse-glows. Download buttons get −2px Y on hover.
